HORIZON PORTAL · LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how Horizon Portal handles information when you visit the website or use connected Horizon services. We use information to provide, secure, and support those services.

1. Who operates Horizon Portal

Horizon Portal is a community project maintained by Praze. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Horizon Portal team through the official Horizon Discord server. Please do not post private account details in public channels.

2. Information we handle

  • Browsing data: hosting and security providers may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser, requested page, and request time to deliver and protect the website.
  • Discord sign-in: if you choose to sign in, the current flow requests your Discord user ID and username. It may also check whether you belong to the Horizon Discord server and which relevant roles you hold. We do not receive your Discord password.
  • Horizon Manager features: connected features may process a local installation identifier, redeemed-code status, access entitlements, and Creator submission details such as project name, game, description, filename, and file size. Files you choose to submit may be checked and stored for review.
  • Cookies: the site may use a session cookie for sign-in, a short-lived cookie to complete Discord authentication, and a language preference cookie when you change language settings.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide pages and account features, authenticate users, check community access, show entitlements, review Creator submissions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and keep the Services secure. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Legal grounds and retention

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the features you request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing a community service, your consent where requested, and legal obligations where applicable. You can stop using optional features and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes, including account access, moderation, security, resolving disputes, and legal requirements. Temporary authentication data is designed to expire. Retention periods vary by data type and provider.

5. Cookies

Cookies support essential sign-in and language preferences. The current site does not intentionally set advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser; disabling essential cookies may prevent some account features from working.

6. Service providers and external links

Discord provides sign-in and community services. Vercel hosts the website and backend. Supabase may provide database or storage services for connected Horizon features. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own policies. External links, including Discord and GitHub, are subject to the destination's privacy practices.

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards apply to international transfers. We disclose information only as needed to run and protect the Services or comply with law.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may be able to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of your personal data, and object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us through the official Discord server to make a request; we may need to verify your identity. The European Commission explains GDPR rights for people in the EU.

8. Children, security, and updates

The Services are not intended for anyone below the minimum age required by applicable law or by a linked third-party service. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when the Services or applicable requirements change; the revision date above shows when it was last updated.
